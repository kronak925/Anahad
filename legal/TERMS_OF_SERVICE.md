## 2. Product Description and Architecture

ANAHAD is an audio playback and media management software client designed for the Android operating system. The Application provides:
1. Playback and library management of audio files stored locally on your device.
2. High-resolution and lossless audio playback visualization (FLAC, ALAC, WAV).
3. Client-side search and playback integration with publicly available third-party online content providers, community lyrics repositories, and content delivery networks.
4. User-directed caching and local storage management of media streams for offline playback.
5. Home screen widget and lock-screen media controls.

ANAHAD does not host, own, store on remote servers, or distribute any proprietary sound recordings or copyrighted music catalog. All indexing, streaming, and caching occur locally on the user's client hardware.
