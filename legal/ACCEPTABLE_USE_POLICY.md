## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") defines the rules and guidelines governing the use of the **ANAHAD** media player application. By using ANAHAD, you agree to comply with this policy.

---

## 2. Permitted Use

ANAHAD is designed and licensed strictly for personal, non-commercial media management and audio playback. Permitted activities include:
1. Playing legally acquired audio files stored locally on your device.
2. Managing personal playlists, favorites, and playback settings.
3. Accessing public metadata, album art, and lyrics for personal listening enhancement.
4. Exporting and restoring personal playback settings and playlists via local backup archives.

---

## 3. Prohibited Activities

You agree NOT to use ANAHAD to:
1. **Infringe Intellectual Property**: Download, store, stream, or distribute audio recordings or musical compositions without legal authorization, license, or applicable fair dealing/fair use rights.
2. **Commercial Exploitation**: Broadcast, redistribute, resell, or publicly perform media obtained through the Application for commercial gain.
3. **Circumvention of Access Controls**: Attempt to bypass, disable, or tamper with Digital Rights Management (DRM), authentication gates, or security protections of third-party platforms.
4. **Service Abuse & Automated Scraping**: Flood external endpoints, APIs, or CDNs with automated scripts, excessive queries, or denial-of-service traffic.
5. **Malicious Tampering**: Distribute modified binaries injected with tracking spyware, adware, malware, or unauthorized background processes.
