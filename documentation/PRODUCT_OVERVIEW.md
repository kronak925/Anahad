# ANAHAD | Product Overview & System Vision

- **Document Version**: 1.0.0
- **Product Title**: ANAHAD (अनहद)
- **Target Platform**: Android Mobile Devices & Tablets
- **Target Audience**: Audiophiles, regional Indian music lovers, privacy-conscious listeners

---

## 1. Product Vision & Problem Statement

Modern commercial music streaming applications have increasingly become congested with invasive advertising, mandatory accounts, intrusive user profiling, bloated social feeds, and restricted audio quality (often capping lossy bitrates at 128kbps or 160kbps). Furthermore, independent local audio files stored on a user's phone are frequently relegated to second-class status, forcing users into segregated interfaces.

**ANAHAD** solves this dichotomy by providing:
1. **Unified Experience**: Equal prominence for offline high-resolution master tracks and online discovery.
2. **True Audio Quality**: Genuine lossless FLAC, ALAC, and WAV playback with clear audio format indicators.
3. **Regional Musical Heritage**: Native language discovery tiles for Hindi, Punjabi, Gujarati, Marathi, Rajasthani, Telugu, Tamil, and more, making regional Indian music instantly accessible.
4. **Total Privacy**: An uncompromising zero-telemetry architecture where listening habits never leave the device.

---

## 2. Core Value Propositions

- **Sound Engineering of Poweramp**: Real-time inspection of decoded audio streams, displaying sample rate and bit-depth metrics (e.g. 24-bit / 96.0 kHz) on verified lossless tracks.
- **Visual Elegance of Apple Music**: Minimalist dark theme typography, blurred adaptive artwork palettes, clean edge-to-edge player controls, and non-distracting navigation.
- **Discovery of Gaana**: Multi-language curated carousels and trending releases tailored to Indian linguistic diversity.
- **Freedom & Control**: Customizable download save destinations, synchronized karaoke lyrics with tap-to-seek, and full offline export/import.
